There are guides online for adding local files to forge mods, but if you host Foundry Locally you need only right click the foundry logo on your task bar, choose "Browse User Data", then open "Data">"Modules" and drag the contents of the zip file into the modules folder. Then you can enable the "Eldritch Dream Creations All In One" in your worlds!

There are three compendiums, each starts with "EDC"

Creatures are in the Actors compendium

Items (and in the future, feats and more) are in the Items Compendium

Journals (including the rules for Spellbound Regalia and Environmental Dangers) are in the journals compendium.
